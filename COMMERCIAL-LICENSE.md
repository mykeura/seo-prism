## FAQ

**Do I need a commercial license to audit my own or my clients' websites?**
No. If you run SEO Prism under the AGPL, you can use it for paid client work. Reports and exports it generates (PDF, JSON, CLI output) are yours and can be delivered to clients freely.

**Does the AGPL force me to publish anything if I run it unmodified?**
No. The source-sharing obligation applies when you modify SEO Prism and offer that modified version to others over a network.

**Does using the CLI, the MCP server or AI agents change anything?**
Not under the AGPL. Under a commercial license, automated use through the CLI, MCP server, scripts, CI or AI agents on behalf of the Licensee counts as the Licensee's own use and does not need extra seats.

**How do I get a license?**
Email mykeura@hotmail.com with your organization name and the plan you want. You will receive an invoice and the commercial license agreement. Paying the invoice or signing the agreement means you accept it. The standard agreement terms are available on request.

**Can I contribute code?**
Yes. Contributions require signing the [CLA](CLA.md), which allows the project to offer SEO Prism under both licenses.
