While a license is active, the Licensee may, according to the purchased plan:

- Modify SEO Prism, integrate it with other systems, and keep all modifications private: the AGPL's requirement to publish modified source code does not apply to the Licensee's modifications during the term.
- Let clients access the Licensee's instance with the Client Access add-on.
- Use the CLI, the MCP server, scripts, CI pipelines or AI agents on its behalf: automated use counts as the Licensee's own use and does not need extra seats.

**Term and renewal.** Each license is valid for 12 months from the date of the signed license agreement (or for the multi-year period purchased). There is no automatic renewal and no automatic charge: renewal is done by repeating the purchase process below. If a license lapses and is renewed later, the new term starts when the new license is issued.

**What happens when the subscription ends.** SEO Prism itself can keep being used under the AGPL-3.0, exactly as before subscribing. The exemption ends and is not renewed automatically. Modifications made while the subscription was active remain the Licensee's work, but from the end of the term they are governed by the AGPL-3.0 again, like the rest of the software: using and adapting them internally stays permitted, but distributing them or offering them to users over a network then requires complying with the AGPL, including publishing the corresponding source code. If you need your modifications to remain private after expiry, renew the subscription before the term ends.

## FAQ

**Can I use SEO Prism commercially without paying?**
Yes. The AGPL allows commercial use. Run it unmodified, or, if you modify it, publish your modifications under the AGPL. You only need the paid license to keep modifications private while offering the modified version to users over a network, or if your organization has a policy against AGPL dependencies.

**What exactly am I buying?**
An annual exemption from the AGPL's source-publication obligation, scoped to the chosen plan and delivered as a signed PDF license. It buys legal certainty for your modifications, not features: nothing is missing from the free version.

**Are there limits in the free version?**
No. The open source edition offers everything: no page caps, no feature gating, no disabled modules. A purchase never unlocks functionality, because none is locked away.

**Does the AGPL force me to publish anything if I run it unmodified?**
No. The source-sharing obligation applies when you modify SEO Prism and offer that modified version to others over a network.

**Can I contribute code?**
Yes. Contributions require signing the [CLA](CLA.md), which allows the project to offer SEO Prism under both licenses.
